# Speedbreaker 1.0.1 verification

This follows the [1.0.0 publication review](2026-10-11-publication.md).
That APK remains a testing prerelease; its artifacts and tag are unchanged.

## Changes and independent review

[PR #7](https://github.com/beejsbj/speedbreaker/pull/7) addresses all three review
findings: committed settings surviving coroutine cancellation (#4), active pause
controls on Home after a dismissed notification (#5), and call interruption
through aggregate Telecom state with an explicit Phone permission setup (#6).
The Back handler also avoids forwarding navigation into a newly exposed safety
screen. Redirects display larger installed-app icons with their labels.

Astra high and Sol 6.1 xhigh independently reviewed the fixes and published their
findings on the PR. Neither confirmed a new product defect. Both required the
initial two failing End now regressions to be resolved before release. The
failure was Robolectric's paused main looper: the queued manifest broadcast
needed dispatch during the test wait. Commit `2b0bbdd` pumps that looper without
changing the UI click, receiver, database, token or expiry assertions.

## Samsung Galaxy S23+ candidate checks

Signed 1.0.1, version code 4, was installed over 1.0.0. Existing Accessibility
consent, the user's weekday 09:00–17:00 schedule and 12-second breath survived.
The new Phone permission was requested explicitly from Home; breaths remained
off until access was granted. The app returned to its ready state afterward.

Calculator was selected temporarily with an always-on override because the
saved global schedule was inactive on Sunday. Opening produced the breath.
A 15-minute pause produced its notification. After a real horizontal swipe
removed that notification, **Active pauses → End now** in the app ended the
pause. A notification-dismiss tool initially reported success while the entry
remained; only the verified physical swipe is counted here.

For the incoming-call check, the user put the phone on silent and called it from
another phone. In the repeat with a fresh opening, the metadata trace showed
the Speedbreaker overlay through 6.661 seconds after the launch call returned.
At 7.682 seconds, Samsung's incoming-call **SYSTEM** window exposed Answer/Decline
and Speedbreaker's overlay was absent. It remained absent while the call rang.
The user separately confirmed that the call interrupted the first eight seconds.
After hangup the visible Calculator received a fresh opening breath.
The first attempt's cue was delayed; it is evidence of yielding, not of timing
inside the initial lock. Polling observations do not establish a hard latency
bound. No caller name, number or call audio was retained in the test trace.

## Scope

Telecom-managed and self-managed calls, plus communication-audio state, supply
the call signal. A calling app exposing neither can evade detection while
ringing. Platform internals can also conceal failures as an idle result.
Permission denial/revocation and exposed query failures suspend breaths.
The nominal polling interval is not a real-time guarantee.

Reboot/recovery, extended idle, other manufacturers and all supported Android
versions have not been checked on hardware for this release. GitHub distribution
does not imply F-Droid, Google Play or App Store approval. See
[distribution](../distribution.md).
