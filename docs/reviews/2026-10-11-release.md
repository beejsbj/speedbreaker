## Phone outcome: installed and verified

Burooj explicitly approved the reviewed APK's per-app Play Protect override.
On the next screen read, Android's normal installer already showed App updated;
no override tap was needed by the agent. App inventory confirmed Speedbreaker
1.0.0/code 3. No safe-scan verdict was shown. Play Protect was left enabled.

Accessibility remained connected and the existing settings survived the update:
a twelve-second breath, always-active hours, and Calculator as the sole temporary
target with a one-minute check-in interval.

Controlled checks on the installed release passed:

- Calculator opening showed the breathing overlay with eleven seconds remaining
  and no choice controls during the hard lock.
- The next observation showed three seconds remaining, Leave and Pause available,
  and Continue still counting down. At completion, Continue was available.
  Exact first-frame unlock timing is covered by the automated frozen-clock test.
- Tapping Continue removed Speedbreaker's overlay and returned focus to Calculator.
- The configured one-minute interval produced a Checking in intervention.
- Leave from that intervention returned Home. Reopening Calculator immediately
  produced a fresh Opening breath with eleven seconds remaining.
- Opening Android Display Settings during that fresh hard lock removed the
  overlay and immediately gave Settings focus.

Cleanup was verified through native UI: Display's Screen timeout summary is
1 minute, and Speedbreaker's home reports Ready when you are, Accessibility is
connected, and Nothing is chosen until you pick it. Calculator was removed;
there are no configured redirects. Burooj will choose his own apps and settings.
The downloaded APK remains available on the phone for sharing.

Task-scoped logs in the artifact directory preserve opening, Continue, interval,
Leave/safety escape, timeout restoration and final handoff observations.
Calls, reboot/recovery, extended idle and other manufacturers' behavior have
not been tested on this release. September's older-build observations remain
historical evidence and are not substituted for release verification.
