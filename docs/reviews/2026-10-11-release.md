## Phone outcome: installation pending owner approval

Android Remote Control MCP is connected on the unlocked Galaxy S23+.
Before the update, Speedbreaker was version 0.1.0/code 1 with Accessibility
connected, a twelve-second breath, always-active hours, and Calculator as its
sole temporary target. Its screen timeout was ten minutes from earlier testing.

The reviewed APK reached Android's normal Update confirmation. Play Protect then
blocked it with this stated reason: it has not seen an app from this developer
before. Expanded details offer a per-app Install anyway link. No safe-scan
verdict was shown, and no override has been selected by the agent. Owner approval
for that specific APK is pending; Play Protect remains enabled.

Release installation and physical opening/countdown/choice/Settings escape
verification are therefore still pending. After those checks, remove the
Calculator target and restore the original one-minute screen timeout before
handoff. Do not treat September's older-build observations as final-release
verification. Calls, reboot/recovery, extended idle and other manufacturers'
behavior have not been tested on this release.

The tracked delivery remains [BJS-141](https://linear.app/bjs-projects/issue/BJS-141/package-sign-and-install-on-buroojs-phone-with-accessibilityservice).
