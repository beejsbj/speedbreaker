## Limits

- Sparse annotated MCP screenshots show the ring and completed layout, but
  cannot establish frame pacing or the absence of small animation jumps.
- Spotify is not installed. Actual audible Instagram Reels behavior was not
  verified; the background-media result is specifically for YouTube.
- Hardware-volume interruption, a controlled incoming notification during the
  lock, and a real incoming call were not tested. The MCP key tool does not
  provide volume keys; the repository's synthetic-call fixture is intended for
  disposable emulators and was not used on the personal phone.
- Pause creation/notification dismissal, redirect selection changes, timed
  check-ins, reboot/recovery and extended idle were not exercised in this pass.
  No daily pause allowance was consumed and no saved settings were changed.
- Existing hosted test results were not rerun for this installation-only pass.

No failure was observed in the completed checks. The original release session's
remaining sensory/interruption checks still need evidence before claiming full
physical-device acceptance.
